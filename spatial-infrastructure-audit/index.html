Econometric Observations

Demographic Slants

Pacific Islander (β = +0.0511), Black (β = +0.0022), and Other Race (β = +0.0153) maintain positive associations with composite disease burden when controlling for income and infrastructure.

Infrastructure Confounding

Trial density returns a negative coefficient (β = -0.0141), reflecting macro-level urban academic clustering rather than individual patient treatment effects.

Spatial Limitation Note

Modifiable Areal Unit Problem (MAUP) applies. County-level aggregation smooths intra-county urban-rural variance.
